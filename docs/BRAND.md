## The single most important observation

**The brochure is LIGHT.** Warm bone paper, not a dark tech deck.

Every competing "future of medicine" site is dark navy with a glowing blue
organ. Staying light is our biggest built-in differentiator — and it is already
the client's own decision, so we are not imposing taste on them.

## Typography
- Display: heavy geometric sans, tight tracking, all-caps
- Theme line: **bold italic**, editorial slant
- Body: clean humanist sans
- **Gap:** no licensed face specified. Needs a real pairing.
